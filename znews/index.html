Creator review policy

How weekly engagement is verified before any later revenue settlement.

Weekly review basis Verified guest engagement Creator logins, self-views, duplicate activity, bots and spam are excluded.

What the weekly report shows

  • Raw views, eligible views, invalid views and spam views.
  • Creator views and self-views are shown separately as excluded activity.
  • Traffic share is calculated only after a completed week is generated.

What can qualify

  • The reader must be a guest, not a logged-in creator.
  • The view must pass minimum reading activity, heartbeat, duplicate, bot and risk checks.
  • Repeated guest activity beyond the allowed window is invalid and cannot load ads.

Review statuses

  • UNDER REVIEW means the snapshot is waiting for an admin decision.
  • APPROVED means the engagement record passed review.
  • HELD means suspicious traffic or an account issue requires investigation.

No Z Sky balance

  • Z Sky 24 does not maintain a creator wallet, credit balance or withdrawal request.
  • This page never calculates or promises a payment amount.
  • Any later revenue settlement will be a separate audited process paid to an eligible Z-Pay account.
Important

Views do not guarantee payment. Only verified advertising revenue and approved legitimate engagement may be used in a later settlement.

Z Sky 24 may hold or reject suspicious traffic and update review rules for fraud prevention, provider requirements or legal compliance.
